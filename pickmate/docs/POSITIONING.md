The [Rime project catalog](https://github.com/rimelabs/rime-dev-projects) was accessible and reviewed before final positioning. This is a comparison of public descriptions, not a source-code audit or proof of uniqueness.

| Close example | Overlap | PickMate's concrete focus |
|---|---|---|
| Continuum | Commerce state survives disconnects; user words resolve interruptions | Exact item/quantity picking with task versions, confirmation delivery rules, atomic inventory decrement, and cancellation-ignoring lookup evidence |
| FieldMate | Hands-free assistance for a physical worker | A stockroom pick rather than computer-repair guidance; one transactional workflow with a reproducible correction/commit stress corpus |
| Rime Voice Demo: AI4 Booth Edition | LiveKit/Rime turn taking and interruptions | A bounded inventory task and durable consequences, with stale-result and duplicate-write acceptance checks |

These examples establish that hands-free assistance, recovery, and interruption are existing themes. Our claim is narrower: correctness of a corrected inventory task across asynchronous speech/tool work and a single durable commit. We do not assert that other implementations lack similar mechanisms.
